Seed and Green Smoothie

  • Flax seeds and/or chia seeds — 15-20 g (about 1.5-2 tablespoons, enough to cover the bottom of a glass to a depth of 1 cm)
  • Water — 200-250 ml (1 glass, filled to the top)
  • Fruit — 1-2 medium bananas or 2-3 peeled kiwis
  • Leafy greens — to taste (for example, a handful—about 30-50 g—of spinach, arugula, or iceberg lettuce)
  1. 1

    Pour the flax seeds and/or chia seeds into a glass to a depth of 1 cm. Fill the glass to the top with water and leave it to soak overnight.

  2. 2

    In the morning, pour the soaked seed-and-water mixture into a blender.

  3. 3

    Add the bananas (or kiwis) and any leafy greens you enjoy, adjusting the amount to taste.

  4. 4

    Blend until it reaches your preferred consistency—whether you like it chunky, medium-textured, or completely smooth.

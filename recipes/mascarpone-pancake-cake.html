Mascarpone Pancake Cake

  • Fermented milk product (kefir, yogurt, or sour milk) — 250 ml (1 cup)
  • Flour — 160-180 g (a little over 1 cup)
  • Sweet pear — 1 medium (about 150 g)
  • Honey or sugar — 1-2 tablespoons (optional)
  • Baking soda — half a teaspoon (optional, for extra fluffiness)
  • Salt — a small pinch
  • Vegetable oil — 30-40 ml (2-3 tablespoons, for frying)
  • Mascarpone (warmed to room temperature) — 200-250 g (about 1 cup)
  • Sour cream — 2-3 tablespoons (optional, for a few of the middle layers)
  • Berries or fresh fruit — to taste (for garnish)
  1. 1

    Grate the sweet pear on a coarse grater (or chop it finely). If it is very juicy, lightly drain or squeeze off the excess liquid.

  2. 2

    In a bowl, whisk together the fermented milk product, honey or sugar (if using), baking soda (if using), and a small pinch of salt. Fold in the prepared pear.

  3. 3

    Add the flour and mix until smooth, thick, and cohesive.

  4. 4

    Warm a thin layer of vegetable oil in a skillet over medium-low heat. Spoon the batter into the pan to form pancakes and cook for 2-3 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through.

  5. 5

    Stack the warm or slightly cooled pancakes, spreading each one with mascarpone warmed to room temperature. Optionally, use sour cream for a few of the middle layers instead.

  6. 6

    Spread the very top layer with mascarpone and garnish as you like, for example with fresh berries or sliced fruit.
